RESEARCH FOCUS 01

Biomedical signal processing

Understanding physiological signals with reliable AI

CORE QUESTION

How can physiological-state models communicate uncertainty and retain performance across people and recording conditions?

BACKGROUND

Why this direction

My biomedical research interests began with EEG analysis during my electrical engineering degree. I later co-authored a study on explainable deep learning for retinal OCT classification. After working with complex imagery and point clouds in industry, I want to bring that engineering experience to robust biomedical learning.

The EEG thesis and OCT publication establish the academic connection. Production AI work adds experience with data quality, visual interpretation and coordinating an engineering team.

RESEARCH INTERESTS
  • EEG representation learning and sleep-state analysis
  • Interpretable medical image classification
  • Cross-subject generalization and calibrated uncertainty
PROPOSED RESEARCH

A testable next step

Start with subject-independent sleep staging, compare spectral-feature and neural baselines, and measure calibration and explanation stability. A later extension to disorders of consciousness would require separate data, appropriate labels and clinical collaboration.

FIRST REVIEWABLE ARTIFACT

A reproducible EEG sleep-staging study with subject-disjoint splits, macro-F1, Cohen kappa, calibration and failure analysis.

This is a proposed study, not a completed result.
