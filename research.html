RESEARCH INTERESTS

Reliable interpretation of geospatial imagery and 3D data

I am interested in methods that connect geometric quality, machine learning and the final spatial information people use. My work in production mapping supplies the practical questions; graduate research would let me test methods that generalize beyond a single workflow.

CURRENT FOCUS

Two connected research areas

Both grow from my experience with orthophotos, point clouds, urban digitization and repeated surveys.

PRIMARY AREA 01

Geospatial AI and GIS

How can GeoAI distinguish real changes in the built environment from differences introduced by acquisition and processing?

Study building-change detection with explicit registration checks and spatially separated evaluation. Connect model outputs to usable map updates and measure both detection quality and review effort.

Read the focused statement →
PRIMARY AREA 02

AI and photogrammetry

How should uncertainty in alignment and reconstruction influence downstream object extraction and map updates?

Evaluate alignment quality together with semantic consistency, then study whether quality-aware selection or weighting reduces mapping errors. Use independent checkpoints and repeat-survey evaluation where suitable reference data are available.

RESEARCH PREPARATION

Engineering and applied AI

I completed Electrical and Electronic Engineering at IUT with a 3.76/4.00 CGPA and first class honours. Since 2021 at HawarIT Limited, I have worked on orthophoto and point-cloud workflows, GIS digitization and building-change analysis. I now lead six AI engineers.

EVALUATION

What I want to measure

A useful method should be assessed across places, acquisition years and sensors. I am particularly interested in independent geometric checks, spatially separated test areas, uncertainty and the time a human needs to review a proposed map update.
