RESEARCH FOCUS 02

Geospatial AI and GIS

Turning geospatial observations into dependable map information

CORE QUESTION

How can GeoAI distinguish real changes in the built environment from differences introduced by acquisition and processing?

BACKGROUND

Why this direction

My work connects the production of geospatial data with the interpretation of that data. I have worked across orthophoto preparation, GIS digitization, point-cloud processing and image-based inspection, and I now lead a team of six AI engineers at HawarIT Limited.

Experience with the source-data workflow helps explain why model failures occur. It also supplies practical questions about map quality, repeat surveys and the cost of manual correction.

RESEARCH INTERESTS
  • Urban building-change detection across acquisition years
  • AI-assisted digitization and map updating
  • Spatial generalization and data-quality-aware learning
PROPOSED RESEARCH

A testable next step

Study building-change detection with explicit registration checks and spatially separated evaluation. Connect model outputs to usable map updates and measure both detection quality and review effort.

FIRST REVIEWABLE ARTIFACT

A spatially evaluated building-change benchmark with error categories, registration sensitivity and map-ready output examples.

This is a proposed study, not a completed result.
