RESEARCH FOCUS 03

AI and photogrammetry

Connecting geometric accuracy with machine perception

CORE QUESTION

How should uncertainty in alignment and reconstruction influence downstream object extraction and map updates?

BACKGROUND

Why this direction

I have worked through the practical stages between captured imagery and final mapping products: AT-related processing, surface and terrain products, rectification, seamlines, tiles, bridge editing and mosaic delivery. My later work expanded into point-cloud processing and alignment across multiple trajectories.

My contribution is practical knowledge of how mapping products are made and corrected. Graduate research would turn those observations into testable methods for reliable geometry and interpretation.

RESEARCH INTERESTS
  • Quality assessment in orthophoto and 3D mapping workflows
  • Multi-pass point-cloud registration and semantic consistency
  • Geometry-informed learning for urban reconstruction
PROPOSED RESEARCH

A testable next step

Evaluate alignment quality together with semantic consistency, then study whether quality-aware selection or weighting reduces mapping errors. Use independent checkpoints and repeat-survey evaluation where suitable reference data are available.

FIRST REVIEWABLE ARTIFACT

A point-cloud alignment case study with residual distributions, geometric reference checks and downstream classification analysis.

This is a proposed study, not a completed result.
