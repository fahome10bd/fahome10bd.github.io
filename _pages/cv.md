Integrated the system into a GIS-based web validation platform, achieving 88% accuracy and reducing manual inspection efforts by 65%.
    </div>
  </div>
  <div class="mcv-entry">
    <div class="mcv-entry-left">City Modeling</div>
    <div class="mcv-entry-right">

Designed custom multi-view texture projection algorithms considering occlusion and view selection, maintaining a texture shift of less than 3 pixels.
    </div>
  </div>
  <div class="mcv-entry">
    <div class="mcv-entry-left">Orthophoto QA</div>

  </div>
  <div class="mcv-entry">
    <div class="mcv-entry-left">Pose Estimation</div>
    <div class="mcv-entry-right">

  <div class="mcv-entry">
    <div class="mcv-entry-left">Remote Sensing</div>
