## Selected Technical & Research Projects

* **3D LiDAR Intelligence & BIM Extraction:** Developed AI algorithms for semantic segmentation and BIM model extraction from large-scale aerial, mobile, and indoor LiDAR datasets. Automated pipe detection and geometric property extraction, achieving **95% extraction accuracy** and reducing manual processing time by **60%**. [Details]({{ '/portfolio/point-cloud-processing/' | relative_url }})
* **360° Street-View GeoAI Localization:** Engineered an end-to-end GeoAI pipeline for automated detection and geospatial localization of **130 traffic sign classes and 33 street furniture assets** from vehicle-mounted 360° imagery using YOLOv5 and custom tracking (**>95% detection accuracy, 92% localization precision**). [Details]({{ '/portfolio/traffic-sign-inspection/' | relative_url }})
* **Multi-Temporal Change Detection:** Developed a deep learning system to detect building extensions, new constructions, and demolitions from multi-year orthophotos. Integrated into a GIS web validation platform, achieving **88% accuracy** and cutting manual inspection efforts by **65%**. [Details]({{ '/portfolio/building-change/' | relative_url }})
* **Large-Scale 3D CityGML Modeling:** Built an automated pipeline for 3D CityGML reconstruction and photorealistic texture generation from high-resolution aerial imagery, designing occlusion-aware multi-view projection algorithms maintaining texture shift **< 3 pixels**. [Details]({{ '/portfolio/gis-digitization/' | relative_url }})
* **Orthophoto Production QA & Optimization:** Automated production workflows by developing AI-based image quality assessment for cloud, shadow, and blur detection with **97% accuracy**. Optimized seamlines and spatial validation using PostgreSQL/PostGIS and AHN LiDAR data, cutting manual editing by **65%**. [Details]({{ '/portfolio/orthophoto-production/' | relative_url }})
* **Aerial Camera Pose Estimation:** Developed a pose estimation framework using feature matching and tie-point generation to align new aerial flights with historical orthophotos, achieving **2-pixel alignment accuracy** and reducing manual aerial triangulation by **90%**.
* **Earth Observation & Remote Sensing:** Deployed AI models on Sentinel multispectral imagery for multi-class crop classification, water quality analysis, and algae bloom detection. Awarded **2nd place in an international innovation tender**. [Details]({{ '/portfolio/remote-sensing/' | relative_url }})
* **Vibration Signal Fault Detection:** Engineered automated machine fault detection using MATLAB signal processing on accelerometer data, identifying and localizing 4 distinct fault types with automated reporting.
* **Cosmic Microwave Background Radiation Analysis:** Processed and denoised fragmented WMAP astronomical datasets to isolate primordial CMBR signals and generate clean sky-maps.
