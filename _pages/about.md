I am a **Team Lead, AI Engineer at HawarIT Limited**, where I lead six AI engineers. My work brings together **computer vision, GIS and photogrammetry**: I develop algorithms and design systems for interpreting aerial imagery, road imagery and point clouds. I completed a BSc in Electrical and Electronic Engineering at the Islamic University of Technology in 2021 with a CGPA of **3.76/4.00** and first class with honours.

## Research interests

I am seeking graduate research in **geospatial AI and photogrammetry**, especially methods that make urban mapping and change detection more reliable across surveys. My work has covered orthophoto production, DSM/DTM workflows, image rectification, GIS digitization, multi-trajectory point-cloud alignment, segmentation, classification and multi-year building-change analysis. This experience motivates questions about geometric quality, spatial generalization and the usefulness of AI outputs to a human reviewer.

My undergraduate thesis investigated EEG signals related to impaired consciousness. I also co-authored a paper on explainable retinal OCT classification. These provide a separate foundation for biomedical signal processing research. Computer vision is established in my professional work; vision-language and LLM research is an intended next direction.
