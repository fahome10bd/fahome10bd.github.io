I am the **Project Lead in the AI Department at HawarIT Limited** with **five years of industrial R&D experience** in **Geospatial AI, 3D computer vision, photogrammetry, and point cloud intelligence**. I lead multidisciplinary teams developing production spatial AI systems for aerial and mobile LiDAR segmentation, automated CAD/BIM extraction, camera pose estimation, orthophoto pipeline automation, 3D CityGML reconstruction, and multi-temporal change detection.

I earned my **B.Sc. in Electrical and Electronic Engineering** from the **Islamic University of Technology (IUT)** with a **CGPA of 3.76/4.00 (First Class Honours)**. My academic foundation in signal processing and interpretable machine learning includes an A+ undergraduate thesis on EEG cerebral rhythms in impaired consciousness and a peer-reviewed **IEEE** publication on explainable deep learning for retinal OCT classification.

I am seeking **M.Sc. / Ph.D. research opportunities** in **3D Scene Understanding, Urban Digital Twins, and Autonomous GIS**—focusing on integrating **neural rendering (3D Gaussian Splatting)** with **structured 3D city modeling (CityGML/BIM)** and **spatiotemporal change detection** for scalable, end-to-end geospatial intelligence.
