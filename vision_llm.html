RESEARCH FOCUS 04

Computer vision and LLMs

Building visual systems that explain what they observe

CORE QUESTION

Can multimodal systems produce useful inspection reports while keeping every factual claim traceable to image or document evidence?

BACKGROUND

Why this direction

My computer-vision experience comes from concrete inspection and mapping tasks: road imagery, changing buildings and point-cloud interpretation. I want to extend this work toward vision-language systems that can describe evidence, support human review and recognize when an image does not justify a conclusion.

Computer vision and engineering leadership are established experience. LLM and vision-language research are a proposed extension; no completed LLM deployment or fine-tuning project is claimed.

RESEARCH INTERESTS
  • Visual understanding under viewpoint and data-quality shifts
  • Grounded vision-language reasoning for inspection
  • LLM evaluation, retrieval and faithful report generation
PROPOSED RESEARCH

A testable next step

Develop a small benchmark of visual observations and reference-grounded questions. Compare a detector-plus-rules baseline, a vision-language model and a retrieval-assisted system; measure factual support, missed defects, abstention and cost.

FIRST REVIEWABLE ARTIFACT

An evidence-grounded visual-inspection benchmark with a fixed test set, baselines, hallucination analysis and reproducible model settings.

This is a proposed study, not a completed result.
