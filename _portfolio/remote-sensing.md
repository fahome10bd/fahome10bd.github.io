Deployed AI solutions leveraging European Space Agency (ESA) Sentinel multispectral satellite imagery for large-scale Earth observation, environmental monitoring, and natural resource management.

### Technical Implementation & Impact
- **Multispectral Analytics:** Engineered computer vision pipelines operating across Sentinel-2 optical and infrared spectral bands to analyze vegetative indices, crop phenology, and surface water dynamics.
- **Environmental Monitoring:** Built multi-class deep learning models for automated crop type classification, water body delineation, and harmful algae bloom detection across aquatic ecosystems.
- **International Recognition:** This Earth observation analytics framework secured **2nd place in an international innovation tender**.

In addition to satellite remote sensing, my open-source computer vision contributions include a multi-label vehicle and traffic detection framework:
- [Multi-Label Vehicle Detection Repository](https://github.com/fahome10bd/Multi-Label-Vehicle-detection-based-on-AI) (comparisons of YOLOv3, YOLOv5, and R-CNN architectures across 21 traffic classes).
