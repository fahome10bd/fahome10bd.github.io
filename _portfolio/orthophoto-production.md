I have worked across the production steps between aerial imagery and a final orthophoto product: AT-related processing, DSM and DTM workflows, image rectification, seamline preparation, tiling, bridge editing and single-file output.

This experience has made me interested in connecting photogrammetric quality with later AI interpretation. A mapping product can appear complete while local geometry or seamline choices affect building extraction or change analysis. A future research study could test which intermediate quality measures best predict those downstream errors.
