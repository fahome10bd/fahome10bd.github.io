My point-cloud work has included processing and alignment across multiple trajectories, followed by segmentation and classification. Alignment quality matters because geometric errors can affect the boundaries and labels of downstream map features.

A research question is whether semantic consistency can help identify local alignment failures that a global registration residual misses. A suitable study would report geometric checks and downstream classification effects separately.
